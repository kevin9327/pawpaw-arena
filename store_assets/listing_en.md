🐾 PawPaw Arena — the cute animal arena shooter

Cats, dogs and pigs tumble and brawl in one arena in a tiny, instant .io game!
Jump straight into a room with other animals, eat snacks, land your shots and grow stronger.

▶ How to play
· Left thumbstick to move, right side to aim and fire
· Eat snacks scattered around the map to level up → pick Damage, Fire rate, Speed or Max HP
· Take down rivals to score. Climb the top‑10 leaderboard
· Respawn 3 seconds after you fall — no pressure, keep going

▶ Three free animals
· 🐱 Cat — fast but squishy, rapid fire
· 🐶 Dog — the balanced all‑rounder
· 🐷 Pig — slow but tanky, hits hard

▶ Premium animals (one‑time unlock)
· 🐰 Rabbit — lightning‑fast glass cannon
· 🦆 Duck — balanced, upgraded
· 🦊 Fox — mid‑range one‑shot sniper

▶ Features
· Play instantly — no tutorial, no sign‑up
· No internet? Play offline against smart bots
· Soft pastel art, cute and cozy
· Short rounds — perfect for a spare minute

Pick your animal and jump into the arena! 🐾
